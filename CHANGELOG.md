# Changelog

All notable changes to Humanizer are documented here.

## [3.8.0] - 2026-09-18

### Added
- Context-aware flow-pattern generator using BPM, meter, lyric stress, rhyme placement, breath load, and neighboring bars.
- Three internal flow strategies per bar: continuity, variation, and contrast.
- Actionable flow descriptions covering subdivision, entry, accent skeleton, phrase shape, and exit behavior.

### Existing capabilities
- AI-writing artifact detection and humanization for prose and lyrics.
- Voice fingerprinting from supplied writing samples.
- Rap scoring for multisyllabic rhyme, pocket, cadence, and rhyme density.
- Score-guided rewriting with frozen strong lines and three-candidate repair tournaments.
- 16-bar verse optimization with local, block, and whole-verse checks.
- Verse-to-hook optimization and transition analysis.
- BPM/time-signature-aware pocket analysis.
