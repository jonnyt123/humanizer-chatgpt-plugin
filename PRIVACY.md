# Privacy policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Humanizer is a skills-only plugin for ChatGPT and Codex. It provides writing, editing, lyric, rap-analysis, and flow-planning instructions and reference material. Humanizer does not operate a publisher-hosted backend, MCP server, analytics service, advertising system, or third-party account connection.

## Categories of personal data

The Humanizer publisher does not independently collect personal data through a Humanizer-operated service.

When a user invokes Humanizer, ChatGPT or Codex may process text the user chooses to provide, including prose, lyrics, writing samples, preferences, and other content included in the request. That content may contain personal data if the user includes it.

Humanizer itself has no independent network service, database, telemetry endpoint, or storage system that receives a separate copy of that content.

## Purposes of use

User-provided content is used by the ChatGPT or Codex product to perform the Humanizer workflow the user requested, such as rewriting text, matching supplied writing characteristics, analyzing lyrics, or planning cadence and flow.

Humanizer does not use user content for publisher-operated advertising, cross-service tracking, profiling, or independent analytics.

## Categories of recipients

Humanizer does not disclose user content to a Humanizer-operated service or to third-party recipients introduced by the plugin.

OpenAI may process conversation and plugin data as part of providing ChatGPT or Codex according to the user's OpenAI account, plan, workspace controls, and applicable OpenAI terms and privacy documentation.

## Data retention

The Humanizer publisher does not retain user prompts, writing samples, generated outputs, or other conversation content through Humanizer because the plugin has no publisher-operated backend, logs, database, or analytics service.

Any retention performed by OpenAI is governed by the user's OpenAI product, account or workspace settings, and applicable OpenAI terms and privacy documentation.

## User controls

Users control what content they provide to Humanizer and may stop using or uninstall the plugin at any time.

Because the Humanizer publisher does not keep a separate copy of user conversation content, there is no publisher-held Humanizer dataset for users to access, correct, export, or delete. Controls for data held by OpenAI are provided through the applicable ChatGPT or Codex account and workspace settings.

## External services, analytics, and tracking

Humanizer v3.8.1 does not require authentication and does not connect to external services. It does not include independent telemetry, advertising trackers, cookies, or analytics code.

If a future version adds an external service, data connection, independent storage, analytics, or materially different data handling, this policy and the public plugin listing will be updated before that capability is published.
