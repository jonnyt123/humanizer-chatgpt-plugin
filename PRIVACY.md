# Privacy policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Humanizer is a skills-only plugin. It provides writing instructions and reference material to ChatGPT and Codex and does not operate an MCP server, connect to third-party accounts, run independent analytics, or send user content to a service operated by the Humanizer publisher.

## Data handled by Humanizer

Humanizer may work with text that a user supplies in the ChatGPT or Codex conversation, including prose, lyrics, and writing samples. The plugin itself has no independent storage or network service, so the publisher does not receive or retain that content through Humanizer.

OpenAI may process conversation and plugin data as part of providing ChatGPT or Codex according to the user's OpenAI account, plan, workspace controls, and applicable OpenAI terms and privacy documentation.

## External services

Humanizer v3.8.1 does not require authentication and does not connect to external services. If a future version adds an external service or data connection, this policy and the plugin listing will be updated before that capability is published.

## Analytics and tracking

Humanizer does not include independent telemetry, advertising trackers, cookies, or analytics code.
