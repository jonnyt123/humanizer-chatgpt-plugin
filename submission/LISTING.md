## Long description
Humanizer rewrites AI-sounding prose and lyrics while preserving meaning, supported facts, concrete detail, and the writer's voice. It can learn a reusable voice fingerprint from supplied writing samples, score lyric artifact risk as an editing diagnostic, analyze rap technique across multisyllabic rhyme, pocket, cadence, and rhyme density, protect strong bars while repairing weak ones, optimize complete verses and verse-to-hook transitions, and use supplied BPM and meter for beat-grid-aware flow planning. Humanizer is skills-only: it has no external account connection, no MCP server, and no independent data collection.

## Capabilities
- Humanize prose without changing supported facts
- Match voice from supplied writing samples
- Score lyric artifact risk as an editing diagnostic
- Analyze rap multis, pocket, cadence, and rhyme density
- Optimize weak bars while preserving strong lines
- Optimize verses, hooks, transitions, and BPM-aware flow

## Starter prompts
1. Humanize this draft while preserving my voice, meaning, and supported facts.
2. Score these lyrics for AI-writing artifacts and rewrite only the weak lines.
3. Analyze this rap verse for multis, pocket, cadence, and rhyme density, then improve weak bars.
